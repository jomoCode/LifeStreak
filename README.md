# Life Streak

Life Streak is a **Todo and adherence tracking application** designed to help users overcome procrastination and stay consistent with their long‑term goals. By breaking big ambitions into small, achievable daily tasks, Life Streak motivates users to build discipline and track progress over time.

---

## Features
- Notification Reminders – Stay on track with timely nudges  
- Adherence & Stats Page – Friendly dashboard to visualize progress and consistency  
- AI Goal Breaker – Break down long‑term goals into tiny, actionable daily steps  
- Motivational Content – Quotes, clips, and scientific summaries that reinforce the value of discipline  

---

## Goal
Enable users to **create, manage, track, and complete tasks efficiently**, while cultivating consistency that leads to mastery.

---

## Platforms
- Mobile: iOS & Android  
- Web: (planned for future expansion)

---

## Problem Statement
Research shows that **mastery comes from consistency**—small efforts repeated over time yield massive results. While most todo apps focus on organization, **Life Streak** emphasizes *adherence* and *discipline*, helping users transform minimal daily actions into long‑term achievements.

---

## Objectives
- Provide a clean and fast task‑taking experience  
- Reduce friction in adding and completing tasks  
- Help users stay organized with simple structuring and easy planning  
- Motivate and incite accountability

---

## Core User Stories
- As a user, I want to set up a goal with a defined time frame  
- As a user, I want to view all my goals and adherence status  
- As a user, I want to drill down into each goal to see pending, missed, and completed tasks  
- As a user, I want to manage my goals (rename, delete, adjust timelines, daily allocations)  
- As a user, I want to see procrastination tendency metrics (e.g., starting late increases likelihood of missing tasks)  
- As a user, I want tasks auto‑sorted by status (completed, pending, missed)
